Get your event on Summit Scene

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Send us your event

Hosting an event, tour, class, or experience in Banff, Canmore, or Lake Louise? Reach out to hello@summitscene.ca. Summit Scene admin reviews submissions and creates listings on behalf of local businesses and organizers.

There are no business or organizer accounts. You do not need an account to request a listing. Optional member accounts are for saving events, liking favourites, and choosing notifications.

What to include

  • Your business or organizer name and contact email.
  • Event title, date, start and end times, town, venue, and address.
  • A short description, price, age restrictions, and accessibility details.
  • An official event website or booking link where people can confirm the details.
  • If you provide a photo, confirmation that you own it or have permission for Summit Scene to use it.

Email your event details

Listing updates and corrections

Admin handles all event publication and edits. If your event changes, is cancelled, or needs a correction, email the listing link and updated details. Submitting an event does not guarantee publication.

Bookings and original sources

Bookings take place on the organizer's external website or booking provider. Summit Scene may also curate factual listings from public event sources, retaining the original source link. Attendees should confirm current details with the organizer.

Event responsibilities

Businesses and organizers remain responsible for accurate event information, cancellations, refunds, safety, and any permits, licences, insurance, venue approvals, or local rules that apply to their activity. A listing on Summit Scene is not an endorsement or approval of an event.

Contact

For listing requests, corrections, and business questions, email hello@summitscene.ca.
