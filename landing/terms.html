Using Summit Scene

By using Summit Scene, you agree to use the app lawfully, respectfully, honestly, and in line with these terms and our Community Guidelines.

Accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Summit Scene may show events at adults-only venues, including bars.
  • Users must agree to Summit Scene's account terms before logging in or creating an account.
  • You must provide accurate account information.
  • You are responsible for keeping your login information secure.
  • You must not impersonate another person, business, venue, organization, or event organizer.
  • Business, organizer, and tour company accounts must only be created by people authorized to represent that business or organization.

User Content

You are responsible for the content you post, including events, tours, plans, groups, notices, replies, profile details, business information, booking links, phone numbers, and images.

  • Do not post harassment, threats, scams, spam, illegal content, unsafe instructions, or misleading event details.
  • Do not post content that violates another person's rights or privacy.
  • Do not post official business, venue, or organizer content unless you are authorized to do so.
  • Summit Scene has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users, including harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, scams, spam, impersonation, or content that targets people based on identity or protected characteristics.

Moderation

Summit Scene may remove content, restrict accounts, reject business verification, or take moderation action when needed for safety, compliance, platform rules, or app integrity.

Users can report objectionable content from posts, replies, events, event posting profiles, and member profiles. Users can block abusive users from member profiles, community posts, and replies. Summit Scene reviews safety reports as quickly as possible and aims to act within 24 hours by removing violating content and restricting, suspending, or removing abusive users when warranted.

Summit Scene helps people discover events, tours, businesses, and community plans. Summit Scene is a discovery platform and is not responsible for third-party tours, events, safety, cancellations, refunds, booking issues, transportation, weather, route conditions, venue operations, or experiences. We do not control user meetups, event operations, venue safety, travel conditions, permits, or organizer decisions. Use your judgment and follow local laws and safety rules.

Saved-event reminders, going-event reminders, and post activity notifications are optional convenience features. Notification timing depends on device settings, operating system behavior, timezone, event details, app activity, and whether notifications are allowed on your device. Always confirm event dates, times, tickets, booking requirements, and venue details yourself.

Purchases, bookings, payments, cancellations, and customer support for those services happen outside Summit Scene and are handled by the organizer or third-party provider.
