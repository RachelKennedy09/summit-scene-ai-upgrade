# Summit Scene: simple badge concept

This is an AI-assisted vector concept authored with editable paths, not a generated photographic image or a claim of hand-drawn authorship. Use it as a starting point for your own drawing and lettering. The original logo and all active app/website assets remain unchanged.

## Files

- `summit-scene-simple.svg`: editable master badge. Letterforms are paths; no font installation is needed.
- `summit-scene-simple.png`: 1200 × 1200 transparent PNG for importing into Procreate or another drawing app.
- `summit-scene-mountain-mark.svg` / `.png`: simplified badge without words for small uses. PNG is 512 × 512 with transparency.

## Four colours

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#173F31` | Lettering, outlines, mountain shadows, trees |
| Cream | `#F6EBD4` | Badge, snow, lettering border |
| Sage | `#82967A` | Mountains |
| Ochre | `#DC9A4C` | Sun |

## Redraw it in your own hand

1. Make a square canvas, ideally 2400 × 2400 pixels or larger. Import the PNG, lower its opacity to about 20%, and lock that reference layer.
2. On a new layer, draw a circle. Use a thick forest outline and a cream fill.
3. Draw one connected silhouette containing the three peaks. Keep the left and middle peaks taller and the right peak lower, following the reference. This concept simplifies the Three Sisters in your original artwork; it is not a surveyed mountain outline.
4. Fill the silhouette with sage. Add one forest shadow to the right side of each peak and one simple cream snow cap. Avoid texture, tiny cracks, and extra contour lines.
5. Draw a single ochre sun behind the peaks. Keep it to one flat circle.
6. Draw three small pines, each made of three stacked triangles and a short trunk. Add one gently curved forest base.
7. Letter SUMMIT above and SCENE below on separate layers. Sketch the letters with a thick round brush. Round every corner, keep the letter thickness similar, and rotate each letter slightly to follow the badge. The small imperfections in your redraw can make it feel more personal.
8. Add a cream border around the lettering, then a thin forest edge. In Procreate, drawing a larger cream silhouette behind the letters works; do not rely on an effect you cannot easily edit.
9. Hide the imported reference. Check your own drawing at a small size, then export a transparent PNG and keep your layered working file.

The SVG separates `badge`, `sun`, `mountain-silhouette`, `mountain-shadow`, `snow-caps`, `foreground`, and `lettering`. These groups can be edited in a vector app. Use the PNG for Procreate; native SVG import is not required.

Use the full badge when SUMMIT SCENE remains readable. Use the mountain mark when the lettering would be too small. Do not stretch either version; keep clear space around it. The concept has not been installed as the production brand or submitted to either app store.
