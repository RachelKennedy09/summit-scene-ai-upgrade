Summit Scene is ready

Thank you for joining the Summit Scene waitlist and supporting this launch.

Summit Scene is a local app for finding events, community plans, groups, jobs and volunteer posts, community notices, and things to do around Banff, Canmore, and Lake Louise.

You can now download the app, browse what is happening nearby, save events, invite a friend, and start connecting with the community.

Download on the App Store

If the button does not open, copy this link into your browser:
https://apps.apple.com/app/summit-scene/id6777819596

What you can do in the app

· Discover events and experiences happening today and this week

· Save events and set reminders

· Browse community posts, groups, jobs and volunteer opportunities

· Post local plans, community notices, and ways to connect
