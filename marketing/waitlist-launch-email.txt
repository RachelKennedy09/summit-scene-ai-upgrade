Subject: Summit Scene is ready to download

Hi there,

Thank you for joining the Summit Scene waitlist and supporting this launch.

Summit Scene is now available to download. It is a local app for finding events, community plans, groups, jobs and volunteer posts, community notices, and things to do around Banff, Canmore, and Lake Louise.

You can use Summit Scene to:

- Discover events and experiences happening today and this week
- Save events and set reminders
- Browse community posts, groups, jobs and volunteer opportunities
- Post local plans, community notices, and ways to connect

Download on the App Store:
https://apps.apple.com/app/summit-scene/id6777819596

Thank you again for being part of Summit Scene from the beginning.
